Faith Mission Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

In short: Faith Mission keeps your information on your own device. We do not run servers that receive your data, and the app has no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Who we are

Faith Mission is a Bible reading app. Contact: ephraimrusike13@gmail.com (ephraimrusike13@gmail.com).

Information the app stores

When you use Faith Mission, the following is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your account: the name and email address you sign up with, an optional bio, and your chosen title. Your password is never stored; the app keeps only a salted, one-way hash used to check it when you sign in.
  • Your reading activity: chapters you mark as read, reading streaks, faith-journey role and milestones, and verses you like or save.
  • Things you write: blog posts, comments and chat messages you create in the app.
  • Settings: such as reminder time and notification preferences.

What we collect and share

Faith Mission does not send any of the information above to us or to anyone else. We do not sell or share personal data, and the app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. The King James Bible text is built into the app, so reading does not require an internet connection.

Device backups

If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup (for example, to your Google account). This is controlled by your device and Google's own policies, not by Faith Mission.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to send reading reminders and milestone messages. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device settings.
  • Run at start-up: to keep scheduled reminders after your device restarts.

Contacting the admin

If you email us, for example about Bishop access, we receive the information in your email (such as your email address and your Faith Mission account email) and use it only to reply to you and provide what you asked for.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account, which removes your account and progress from the device. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.

Children

Faith Mission is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because data stays on the device, we do not hold any data about any user.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information (for example, if online accounts are added), we will update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect.
