Faith Mission Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 (updated for online accounts, blog and chat)

In short: To use Faith Mission you create an account with your name and email. Your account, public profile, follows, and the blog posts, comments and chat messages you write are stored with Google Firebase so other members can see them. Your reading progress is backed up privately to your account so it follows you to new devices. There is no advertising, analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Faith Mission is a Bible reading and community app. Contact: ephraimrusike13@gmail.com (ephraimrusike13@gmail.com).

Information we collect and why

  • Account: your email address and password, used to sign you in and to send password-reset emails. They are handled by Firebase Authentication (Google); passwords are never visible to us.
  • Public profile: your name, and optionally a bio and title, plus your faith-journey role (for example Believer or Bishop). Other members can see your profile. Your email is not shown to other members.
  • Things you post: blog posts, comments, likes and chat messages. Other signed-in members can see them, with your name.
  • Following: who you follow and who follows you. Other members can see follower and following counts, and whether you follow them.
  • Reading progress backup: chapters you have read, streaks and milestones, your saved and liked verses, bookmarks, the people you block, and your app settings are backed up to your account so they are the same on all your devices. Only you can see this backup.
  • Reports: if you report content, we receive your report and its reason so the admin can review it.

We use this information only to run the app's features. We do not use it for advertising and do not sell or rent it.

Who processes your data

Accounts, profiles and community content are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes them on our behalf under Google's terms. Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by security rules: for example, only you can edit your profile or delete your own posts and messages.

Device backups

If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup (for example, to your Google account). This is controlled by your device and Google's own policies, not by Faith Mission.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to send reading reminders and milestone messages. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device settings.
  • Run at start-up: to keep scheduled reminders after your device restarts.

Contacting the admin

If you email us, for example about Bishop access, we receive the information in your email and use it only to reply to you and provide what you asked for.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This deletes your sign-in account, your public profile, your reading-progress backup and the account data on your device, and removes you from the follower lists of everyone you follow. You can delete any of your own posts, comments and chat messages in the app before deleting your account; to have any remaining content removed, email us and we will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

Children

Faith Mission is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect.
